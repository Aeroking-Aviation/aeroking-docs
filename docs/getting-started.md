# Getting started

This page covers the essentials that are the same no matter which role you hold —
signing in, finding your way around and looking after your own account. Once you're
comfortable here, head to **your [role manual](dashboard/roles/index.md)** for the
day-to-day detail.

## Signing in

Every flying school has its own private web address (portal), usually in the form:

```
https://<your-school>.aerokingaviation.com
```

!!! note "Your school's address"
    Your school chooses its own short name (its *subdomain*), so your link might look
    like `https://highland.aerokingaviation.com`. Your school will send you the exact
    link. Bookmark it — it's your front door to AeroKing.

1. Open your school's portal link.
2. Enter the **email address** your school registered for you and your **password**.
3. Select **Log in**.

You'll land on your personal **home dashboard**, which is tailored to your role.

### First-time sign-in

If an administrator has just created your account, you'll usually receive an email
inviting you to set a password. Follow the link in that email to choose your password,
then sign in as above.

### Forgotten password

1. On the login screen, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and submit.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link and choose a new password.

!!! warning "No reset email?"
    Check your spam folder first. If it still hasn't arrived, your email address may not
    be the one on file — ask your school's administrator to confirm it, or to send you a
    fresh invite.

## Finding your way around

AeroKing uses a single top navigation bar. **What you see there depends on your role and
the permissions you've been given** — you'll only ever see the areas you're allowed to
use, so your menu may be shorter than a colleague's. That's normal.

| Element | Where | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| **School logo** | Far left | Returns you to your home dashboard |
| **Menu items** | Centre | Your areas — some open a dropdown of related pages |
| **Badges** | On a menu item | A number means items need attention (e.g. pending requests) |
| :material-bell: **Notifications** | Top right | Alerts for bookings, approvals, expiries and messages |
| **Your name** | Top right | Opens **My Settings** (your profile) |
| **Log out** | Top right | Signs you out securely |

!!! tip "On a phone or tablet?"
    The menu collapses into a **☰ hamburger** button in the top corner. Tap it to open
    the full menu; it closes automatically when you pick a page.

### Notifications

The :material-bell: **bell** in the top-right corner shows alerts relevant to you — a new
booking, an approval you requested, a document about to expire, and so on. A red dot means
there's something unread. Select the bell to read and clear them.

### The Assistant and feedback

On most pages you'll see two floating buttons in the bottom corner:

- **Assistant** — an in-app help chat for quick "how do I…?" questions.
- **Feedback** — report a problem or suggest an improvement directly to the AeroKing team.

These are hidden on printable pages (invoices, vouchers, statements and the public lobby
board) so they don't appear on your printouts.

## Looking after your account

Select **your name** (top right) to open **My Settings**. Here you can:

- Update your **contact details** (phone, address, emergency contact).
- Change your **password**.
- Set your **notification preferences**.

!!! info "Who can change what"
    You can always edit your **own** profile. Changing someone **else's** details, or
    anything about memberships, roles and billing, is an administrator task — see the
    [Administrator manual](dashboard/roles/admin.md).

## Signing out

Always select **Log out** (top right) when you're finished on a shared or public computer.
AeroKing fully resets when you log out, so the next person can't see your information.
