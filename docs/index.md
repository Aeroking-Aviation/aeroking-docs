# Welcome to the AeroKing Help Centre

AeroKing is a family of connected products for flying schools, clubs and aviation
businesses. These guides are written for the people who use AeroKing every day —
students, instructors, front-desk staff, engineers and administrators — in plain
English, with step-by-step walkthroughs.

!!! tip "New here? Start with the basics"
    If this is your first time signing in, read **[Getting started](getting-started.md)**
    first. It covers logging in, finding your way around and setting up your account —
    and applies to every AeroKing product.

## Find your role

Most people only need the manual for **their** role. Jump straight to yours:

| If you are a… | You'll want |
|---------------|-------------|
| Student, trial-flight customer or qualified pilot renting aircraft | [Student & Pilot manual](dashboard/roles/student.md) |
| Flying instructor | [Instructor manual](dashboard/roles/instructor.md) |
| Front-desk / operations staff | [Front Desk / Staff manual](dashboard/roles/staff.md) |
| Aircraft engineer / maintenance | [Engineer manual](dashboard/roles/engineer.md) |
| School administrator / owner | [Administrator manual](dashboard/roles/admin.md) |

Not sure what you can do? The **[Capabilities & permissions](dashboard/reference/capabilities.md)**
page explains how access works, and the **[role matrix](dashboard/roles/index.md)** shows
who can see what.

## Can't find an answer?

- Check the **[Dashboard FAQ](dashboard/faq.md)** for common questions.
- Use the **search box** at the top of any page.
- Still stuck? Use the **in-app Assistant** (the chat bubble in the bottom corner of the
  dashboard) or the **Support** link in your portal's footer.
