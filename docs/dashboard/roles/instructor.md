# Instructor manual

As an instructor you teach and assess. The Dashboard gives you your diary, your students
and their progress, the tools to grade lessons and ground sessions, and your own flight
logbook and documents.

## Your menu

| Menu | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| **Home** | Your day at a glance — today's lessons and recent activity |
| **Bookings Calendar** | The shared school diary; your bookings are highlighted |
| **My Students** | The students assigned to you, and their progress |
| **My Flight Logs** | Your own pilot logbook (instructor time, dual given, etc.) |
| **Courses** | The syllabus and course material for your students |
| **Assessments** | Grade flight lessons and ground sessions |
| **My Documents** | Your certificates and personal documents |
| **Office** | Your leave, policies, compliance and the team calendar |
| **My Settings** | Your profile and preferences |

## Key tasks

### See today's lessons

Your **Home** dashboard opens on today. For the full diary, open **Bookings Calendar** —
your own bookings are highlighted. Select any booking to see its detail (student, aircraft,
time, notes).

### Grade a flight lesson

1. Open the lesson from your **Bookings Calendar** or **My Students**, or go to
   **Assessments → new**.
2. Record the **exercises** covered and a **grade** for each.
3. Add **debrief notes** and the **weather / conditions**.
4. Save. The student's **progress** and the course record update immediately, and the
   student can read the debrief in their own portal.

### Grade a ground school session

Use **Assessments → ground assessment** for ground-school or briefing sessions. It works
the same way as a flight assessment but is tailored to ground exercises.

!!! info "Why can I grade but an admin can't?"
    Grading is controlled by the *submit assessments* capability, which instructors hold by
    default. An owner can grant it to others too — so don't be surprised if a senior admin
    can also grade.

### Track a student's progress

1. Go to **My Students** and open a student.
2. You'll see their position against the syllabus, completed exercises, grades and hours.
3. Use this before a lesson to pick up where they left off, and after to confirm the record
   updated.

### Record your own flying

Your **My Flight Logs** page is your personal pilot logbook — PIC, dual given, instructor
time, night/IFR/cross-country conditions and landings, per aircraft. You can print it from
**Pilot's Logbook → print** for your paper records or a licence application.

### Book leave or read a policy

Open the **Office** menu for self-service HR: request **leave**, check the **team
calendar**, read **policies** and keep your **certifications** and **compliance** items up
to date. Your leave requests go to whoever holds HR administration (usually an
administrator) for approval.

## Tips

- **Grade promptly after the flight** while the debrief is fresh — the student sees it and
  their progress stays accurate.
- **Check the student's progress before each lesson** so you start at the right exercise.
- **Keep your own certifications current** in the Office area; expiries are tracked and
  flagged.
