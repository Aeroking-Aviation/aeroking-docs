# Engineer manual

Engineers keep the fleet airworthy. Your home is the **Fleet** — the aircraft register,
their maintenance logs and their documents. Your menu is built almost entirely from your
[capabilities](../reference/capabilities.md), so an owner can give you access to additional
operational areas (like the calendar or members) as your role requires.

## Your menu

| Menu | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| **Fleet** | The aircraft/simulator register, maintenance and aircraft documents (your home) |
| **Schedule** | *(if granted)* Bookings calendar and pending requests |
| **Members** | *(if granted)* The people directory |
| **Finance** | *(if granted)* Billing and vouchers |
| **Office** | Your leave, policies and compliance self-service |
| **My Settings** | Your profile and preferences |

!!! info "Fleet is always yours; the rest are optional"
    By default you hold *view & edit the fleet, maintenance and the aircraft logbook*, plus
    HR and compliance self-service. Anything else above only appears if an owner has granted
    you that capability.

## Key tasks

### Log maintenance on an aircraft

1. Open **Fleet** and select the aircraft.
2. Go to its **maintenance log**.
3. Add an entry and choose the type — **squawk**, **scheduled 50-hour**, **annual**,
   **ARC renewal**, **AD compliance** or **repair**.
4. Record the detail (work done, hours, parts, sign-off) and save.

The log is the aircraft's maintenance history; keeping it current is what makes the fleet's
airworthiness status trustworthy.

### Keep airworthiness and expiries current

On each aircraft's detail page, maintain:

- **Airworthiness**, **insurance**, **radio licence** and **pitot-static** expiry dates.
- **Servicing reminders** — by calendar date and by **Hobbs hours** — with custom labels
  and lead times.

AeroKing flags items as they approach due, so you can plan work before an aircraft would
otherwise be grounded.

### Record an aircraft's hours

Keep the aircraft's **Hobbs / Tach** readings up to date so hours-based service reminders
stay accurate and flights reconcile against the logbook.

### Manage aircraft documents

Certificates and airframe records live with the aircraft. Upload new versions as they're
renewed — expiry tracking then covers them alongside the reminders above.

### Your own leave and compliance

Use the **Office** menu for self-service: request **leave**, read **policies** and keep
your own **compliance** and **certifications** current.

## Tips

- **Log work as you do it** — a maintenance history is only useful if it's complete and
  current.
- **Act on reminders early** — a scheduled 50-hour or ARC renewal is far easier to plan
  ahead than to scramble for once an aircraft is grounded.
- **Need the calendar or members list?** Ask your owner/administrator to grant the
  capability — the menu item appears as soon as they do.
