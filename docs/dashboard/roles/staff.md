# Front Desk / Staff manual

Staff keep the front desk and operations running: the booking diary, visiting-aircraft
handling, taking payments and looking after documents. Your access is operational — you
help everything run smoothly without needing the full administrator toolkit.

!!! info "Your menu may vary"
    Staff access is heavily [capability](../reference/capabilities.md)-based, so an owner
    can tailor exactly what your front desk can do. The items below are the common defaults;
    yours may differ.

## Your menu

| Menu | Pages | What it's for |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Home** | Staff dashboard | The day's snapshot: bookings and things to action |
| **Schedule** | Bookings Calendar · Schedule · Pending Requests | Run the diary and approve requests |
| **Handling** | PPR console *(add-on)* | Visiting-aircraft requests and parking |
| **Finance** | Billing & Accounts · Vouchers & Packages | Take payments, raise invoices, sell vouchers |
| **Office** | Leave, policies, compliance, directory | Your own HR self-service |
| **My Settings** | Your profile | Your account |

## Key tasks

### Confirm a booking request

1. Open **Schedule → Pending Requests** (the badge shows how many are waiting).
2. Check the aircraft, instructor and time.
3. **Confirm** to place it on the calendar, or **Decline** with a reason.

The system blocks clashes automatically, so you can't confirm a slot that overlaps an
existing aircraft or instructor booking.

### Build or adjust the day's schedule

Use **Schedule → Schedule** to lay out and adjust the day — assign aircraft and instructors
and resolve clashes. **Bookings Calendar** is the shared read/colour view everyone works
from.

### Check a visiting aircraft in and out (Handling)

!!! note "Add-on"
    **Handling / PPR** only appears if your school has enabled the add-on.

1. Open **Handling**.
2. Review incoming **PPR requests** (permission to land) from visiting pilots and
   **approve** or **decline** them.
3. On arrival, **check the aircraft in** and assign a **parking spot**.
4. On departure, **check it out** — parking fees are calculated from the rate card.

Visiting pilots can submit their own request through your school's public
**parking-request** link, which lands in this queue.

### Take a payment or raise an invoice

1. Go to **Finance → Billing & Accounts**.
2. Find the member (or choose **ad-hoc** for a walk-in).
3. Add line items and issue the invoice, then record how it was paid.

### Sell or redeem a gift voucher

Go to **Finance → Vouchers & Packages** to sell a voucher (e.g. a trial-flight experience)
or redeem one against a booking. Vouchers can be printed on a branded template for the
customer.

### Your own leave and policies

The **Office** menu is your self-service HR: request **leave**, read **policies**, keep
your **compliance** items current and use the **staff directory**. Approvals go to whoever
holds HR administration.

## Tips

- **Clear the Pending queue regularly** — members are waiting on confirmation to fly.
- **Check visiting aircraft out before they leave** so parking is billed correctly.
- **Use the member's statement** to answer "what do I owe?" — it's the definitive record.
