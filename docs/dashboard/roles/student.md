# Student & Pilot manual

This manual covers everyone in the "member" side of the Dashboard who flies or learns:

- **Students** — training towards a licence or rating (the full experience).
- **Qualified pilots** — licensed pilots who hire/rent aircraft but aren't in training.
- **Social members** — club members who don't fly.

Most of this page is written for **students**; the
[Qualified pilot](#qualified-pilot-differences) and [Social member](#social-member) sections
below note what's different for them.

## Your menu (student)

| Menu | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| **Home** | Your next lesson, account balance and anything needing attention |
| **My Calendar** | Request and view your bookings |
| **My Courses** | Your training course(s), lessons and materials |
| **My Progress** | How far you are through the syllabus |
| **Quizzes** | Knowledge quizzes set for you |
| **Pilot's Logbook** | Your flight log, with a printable version |
| **My Documents** | Your certificates, medical, licences, etc. |
| **My Account** | Membership, balance and transaction history |
| **My Settings** | Your profile and preferences |

## Key tasks

### Request a lesson or booking

1. Open **My Calendar**.
2. Pick the day and time you'd like to fly.
3. Choose the **booking type** (e.g. lesson) and, if offered, a preferred aircraft or
   instructor.
4. Submit. Your request goes to the school as **pending**.

!!! note "Pending vs confirmed"
    A request isn't a confirmed flight until the school approves it. You'll get a
    :material-bell: notification when it's **confirmed** (or if it's declined). Confirmed
    bookings show on your calendar.

### Check what's coming up

Your **Home** dashboard shows your next booking. **My Calendar** shows everything —
select any booking to see its details, and to cancel if your school's rules allow it.

### Follow your training progress

- **My Courses** lists your course(s); open one to see its lessons and materials.
- **My Progress** shows how far you've come through the syllabus, lesson by lesson.
- After each flight, your instructor grades the lesson and adds a debrief — you'll see the
  grade and notes here.

### Do a quiz

Open **Quizzes** to see any knowledge quizzes set for you, complete them online and see
your results.

### View and print your logbook

**Pilot's Logbook** is your running flight record. Use **Pilot's Logbook → print** for a
clean, printable copy for your paper logbook or a licence application.

### Check your account and balance

Open **My Account** to see your **membership**, current **balance** (prepaid credit or an
amount outstanding) and a history of **charges, payments and refunds**. Invoices your
school raises appear here too.

### Keep your documents up to date

**My Documents** holds your medical, licence, ID and any school records. Upload new
versions when things are renewed — expiry dates are tracked so you and the school get a
reminder before anything lapses.

## Qualified pilot — differences

Qualified pilots use the **same area as students, minus training**. You get:

- **Home**, **My Calendar**, **Pilot's Logbook**, **Quizzes**, **My Documents**,
  **My Account** and **My Settings**.

You **don't** see **My Progress** or **My Courses**, because you're not working through a
training syllabus. Booking an aircraft, keeping your logbook, managing documents and your
account all work exactly as described above.

!!! tip "Hiring an aircraft"
    Request the aircraft and time from **My Calendar** just like a lesson — choose the
    **rental** booking type if your school offers it. The flight still posts to your
    logbook and your account afterwards.

## Social member

Social (non-flying) members have the simplest view — **My Account** and **My Settings**
only. Use **My Account** to see your **membership status**, renewal date and any
transactions. There's no calendar, logbook or training, because a social membership doesn't
include flying.

---

## Tips

- **Request early** — popular slots and instructors fill up; the sooner you request, the
  better the chance of your preferred time.
- **Watch your balance** — top up before a block of lessons so a low balance never holds up
  a booking.
- **Keep your medical and licence current** — upload renewals as soon as you have them.
