# Digital forms & waivers

Build your own waivers, consents and declarations, and have people sign them in person on a
tablet. The signed PDF is stored against the member or voucher. Manage templates under
**Settings → Forms** (`forms.manage`).

## Build a form

1. Open **Settings → Forms** and create a **template**.
2. Add your fields and wording (e.g. an assumption-of-risk waiver, a photo consent).
3. Save — it's now available to sign.

## Sign in person

On a tablet at the desk, launch the form for the person to read and sign. The completed,
signed **PDF is saved to their record** — against the member, or against a voucher recipient.

!!! tip "Vouchers → members"
    When you turn a voucher recipient into a full member, their signed form comes across with
    them, so you don't re-collect it.
