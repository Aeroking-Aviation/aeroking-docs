# Your plan & licence

Owners and administrators can see what the school is subscribed to under **Settings → Licence**
(`settings.manage`).

## What's on the page

- **Plan** — your base price and how many aircraft are included; extra aircraft bill per
  aircraft.
- **Aircraft** — how many you're using versus your **allowance**.
- **Add-ons** — the extras active for your school (e.g. Handling, Email Centre), with any trial
  dates.
- **Next renewal** and the **billing account** it's billed to.
- **Agreement** — if AeroKing has uploaded your signed agreement, download it here.

## Aircraft allowance

Your plan includes an aircraft allowance. On the **Fleet** page the counter shows how many of
your allowance you're using.

??? question "Why can't I add another aircraft?"
    You're at your plan's allowance — **Add Aircraft** is disabled at the limit. Remove an
    aircraft, or contact AeroKing to raise the allowance.

??? question "What happens if we go over?"
    If your fleet exceeds the allowance (for example after a plan change), the Fleet page warns
    you and, after a grace period, the newest excess aircraft are taken out of service until
    you're back within the plan. You choose which to keep. Contact AeroKing to raise the
    allowance instead.
