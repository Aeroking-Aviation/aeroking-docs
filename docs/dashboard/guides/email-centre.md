# Email Centre

A bulk-mailing add-on for reaching your members. It needs the **Email Centre** add-on enabled
for your school and the `comms.view` / `comms.manage` capability. Find it under **Email Centre**.

## Mailing lists

Build a list two ways:

- **Dynamic** — a live member filter (e.g. "active students"). It keeps itself up to date as
  members change.
- **Static** — a hand-picked set of people.

## Compose

On **Compose**, pick a list (or an ad-hoc filter), then write your email:

- Reusable **templates** save a subject + body you use often.
- **Merge fields** personalise each email (e.g. the recipient's first name).
- Send a **test** to yourself first.
- **Send now**, or **schedule** it for a date and time.

Unsubscribes are handled for you — recipients who opt out are suppressed from future sends.

## Campaigns (after sending)

Open a campaign to see **delivery, opens and clicks**, and:

- **Resend to failed** — retry just the recipients who bounced or failed, in one click.
- The **recipient log** defaults to the ones needing attention (failed/bounced); filter or
  search it, or **export the whole list to CSV** rather than loading thousands of rows.
