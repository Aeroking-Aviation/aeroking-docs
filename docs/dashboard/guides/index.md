# Feature guides

Step-by-step guides for each area of the Dashboard. These complement the
[role manuals](../roles/index.md) (which group tasks by who you are) by going deeper on a
single feature — useful whatever your role.

!!! info "Screenshots coming"
    These guides are written text-first. Annotated screenshots will be added once a demo
    school is set up, so the pictures show realistic data.

- [Handling / PPR](handling-ppr.md) — visiting aircraft, the rate card, the airport summary
- [Fleet & maintenance](fleet-maintenance.md) — the register, the maintenance hub, airworthiness
- [Training, courses & assessments](training.md) — courses, lessons, quizzes, grading, progress
- [Safety reporting](safety.md) — occurrences, hazards and the safety register
- [Office, HR & compliance](office-hr.md) — leave, policies, certifications, the audit programme
- [Email Centre](email-centre.md) — mailing lists and bulk email (add-on)
- [Digital forms & waivers](forms.md) — build and sign forms on a tablet
- [Rooms & facilities](rooms.md) — list and book your spaces
- [Importing your data](importing.md) — bring an existing school across (owner)
- [Your plan & licence](licence.md) — plan, aircraft allowance, add-ons, renewal
- [Notifications & email settings](notifications.md) — channels and school-wide controls
- [Usage reports](reports.md) — how your team and students use the system
