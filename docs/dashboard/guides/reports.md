# Usage reports

See how your team and students are actually using the system, under **Reports**
(`reports.view`).

## What it shows

- **Page views** and **key actions** over time — which areas are getting used.
- Friction signals that hint where people get stuck.

It's **first-party and private to your school** — this is your own usage, not third-party
tracking, and it isn't shared.

!!! tip "Why it's useful at rollout"
    In the first weeks after go-live, Reports is a quick way to see whether the office and
    students are actually picking up the new workflows (bookings, self-booking, logging) — and
    where a nudge or a bit of training would help.
