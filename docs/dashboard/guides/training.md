# Training, courses & assessments

How a student's training is structured, delivered and graded.

## Courses & lessons

Training is two layers:

- **Syllabus** — the global training frameworks.
- **Courses** — your school's own courses built on the syllabus, each with **lessons**, notes
  and materials. Set these up under **Courses** (`courses.*`).

Students see their courses in **My Courses** and work through the lessons.

## Enrolment

Enrol a student onto a course from the admin/instructor course view (or bring enrolments across
at [import](importing.md) — they attach to courses that already exist).

## Ground school coverage

From a **ground school booking**, the instructor marks which lessons were covered and pins the
agenda; reusable session templates can pre-fill a booking's aims. Coverage feeds the student's
progress.

## Quizzes

Lessons can carry **quizzes** (multiple-choice / true-false). Students take them inside the
lesson, get scored with explanations, and **passing a practice quiz can tick off** that lesson's
progress.

## Grading an assessment

After a lesson, the instructor completes an **assessment** — reached from the booking's
*Resume / View Assessment*, or **Assessments → New**. On the form:

1. **Flight details** — date, student, aircraft, route/times/landings (add a sector per leg).
2. **Technical log** — airframe/engine meters (Auto-fill estimates, always check the actual
   meter).
3. **Lessons covered** — grade each exercise; it flows to the student's progress.
4. **Overall grade** and **debrief notes**.
5. **Mark Complete** (or **Save Draft** to finish later).

Completing it updates the student's **progress** automatically. Drafts are surfaced on the
Assessments list so the loop gets finished. See the
[instructor manual](../roles/instructor.md#grade-a-flight-lesson).

## Following progress

Instructors see a student's progress under **My Students**; students see their own under
**My Progress** — exercises covered, grades, hours and what's next.
