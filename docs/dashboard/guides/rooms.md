# Rooms & facilities

List your classrooms, briefing rooms and other spaces, and — if you want — make them bookable
alongside aircraft and instructors. Manage them under **Settings → Rooms & Facilities**
(`rooms.view` to see, `rooms.manage` to edit).

## Add a room

1. Open **Settings → Rooms & Facilities**.
2. Add the room with a name and any details.
3. Tick **bookable** if people should be able to reserve it.

## Booking a room

A bookable room appears as a **resource on the booking form**, so you can reserve it like an
aircraft or instructor. Rooms don't get their own lane on the calendar — they're picked on the
booking itself.
