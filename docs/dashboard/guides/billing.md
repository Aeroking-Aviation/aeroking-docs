# Billing & payments

Member accounts, invoices, payments and credit notes live under **Finance → Billing &
Accounts** (`billing.*` capabilities). Students see a read-only view of their own account.

## The member ledger

Each member has an **account** with a running balance. Charges and payments post to the
ledger; the balance is the sum of everything on it. Un-invoiced entries can be edited or
removed; entries already on an invoice are protected.

## Invoices

1. Open a member (or **raise an ad-hoc invoice** for a non-member / walk-in).
2. Add one or more **lines** — each carries its own VAT rate.
3. **Issue** it to assign a number and a due date.
4. **Mark it paid** when the money arrives.

## Payments & part-payments

Record a payment against an account; it moves the balance and clears matching charges. You can
record money in **instalments** — a **part-payment** — with the outstanding balance tracked.

## Credit notes

- **Full** — close out an invoice entirely (then reissue a corrected one).
- **Partial** — credit just the disputed lines.

## Statements

A member's **statement** is the definitive record of charges, payments and credit — produce it
for a formal breakdown or a query.

!!! note "Owing members can't be deactivated"
    A member who owes money can't be deactivated until the balance is settled — a guard against
    losing track of a debt.
