# Handling / PPR

Handling (Prior Permission Required) manages **visiting aircraft** — landing requests, parking,
and the charges that go with them. It's an **optional add-on**; if it's off, the menu doesn't
appear. Enable it in School Settings. See also the
[staff manual](../roles/staff.md#check-a-visiting-aircraft-in-and-out-handling).

## The request flow

1. A visiting pilot submits a request through your school's public **parking-request** link
   (or staff raise one from the new-request form).
2. It arrives in the **Requests** queue. Pick a free spot for the dates and **Approve**.
3. On arrival, **Check in**.
4. In **Charges**, click **Suggest** to auto-fill rate-card lines by movement type, weight band
   and nights; adjust and **Save**.
5. **Bill** it — members are charged to their ledger; visitors get an issued invoice.
6. On departure, **Check out** to free the spot.

## The rate card

Every priced item (handling, landing/nav, parking, fuel…) lives on the **Rate card** tab.
Set a retail price and VAT% (the net is worked out) and a category so **Suggest** can pick it
automatically. Inactive items drop out of Suggest but stay on old records.

## Airport summary & invoicing

- A weekly **landing summary** you review and send to the airfield (with CSV / print), from
  your school's **Airport** settings.
- Where your school acts as the airport's agent, the **PPR summary + airport reconciliation**
  tools remit collected fees and raise the airport's service-fee invoice.

Settings for Handling live under **Settings → Parking Fees**.
