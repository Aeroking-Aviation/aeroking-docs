# Notifications & email settings

AeroKing keeps people informed with an in-app notifications inbox and email reminders, and
gives the school control over which emails go out.

## Your notifications

- An in-app **inbox** collects booking reminders, document/waiver expiry, membership renewals
  and inactivity nudges.
- Choose **which channels** you get (in-app and/or email) under **your name → My Settings**.

## School-wide email control (admin)

Administrators control outbound email for the whole school under **Settings → Emails**:

- A **master switch**, plus **per-category** toggles — bookings, reminders, PPR, HR, billing.
- Turning a category off stops those emails for **everyone**.
- **Account & security** emails (sign-in, password) always send, regardless.

Members see school-disabled options greyed out in their own My Settings, so it's clear when a
category has been switched off centrally.

!!! note "Sender & reply-to"
    School emails are sent on your school's behalf with a reply-to back to the school, and carry
    the appropriate footer. The exact sender is configured per deployment by AeroKing.
