# Office, HR & compliance

The **Office** area (menu: **Office**, routes under **HR**) covers staff admin — leave,
policies, certifications and the compliance audit programme. Most pages need the `hr.view`
capability; approving and configuring needs `hr.manage`.

## Leave & sick days

- **Request leave:** open **HR → Leave**, choose your dates (your remaining allowance shows),
  and submit. A manager approves or declines and you're notified.
- **Sick days** are logged on the same page.
- **Managers** (`hr.manage`) approve/decline, set allowances, and see who's off on the
  **Team Calendar**.
- A printable leave form is available for your records.

## Policies & procedures

The **Policies** centre holds company policies with **staff acknowledgements** — staff tick to
confirm they've read each one, and managers can see who has outstanding acknowledgements.

## Certifications & work permits

- **Certifications** tracks ratings, medicals, DBS and similar, each with an expiry and
  automatic **reminder emails**.
- **Work permits** are tracked the same way, with expiry reminders.

## Directory, emergency contacts & noticeboard

- A **staff directory** with roles and contact details, including **emergency contacts**.
- A company **noticeboard** for announcements to the team.

## Compliance monitoring programme

For ATO/audit obligations, the **Compliance** programme is an annual schedule (audit areas ×
months) with assignees. Due items are surfaced to the assignee and admins via on-login alerts,
a calendar strip and email reminders, and show on the **Team Calendar**. Needs
`compliance.view` / `compliance.manage`.
