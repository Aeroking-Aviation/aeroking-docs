# Bookings & the calendar

The calendar is the heart of day-to-day operations — lessons, rentals, trial flights, ground
school, maintenance and staff blocks all live here.

## The calendar

Each bookable person or aircraft has its own column. Green **Available** slots are clickable;
AeroKing prevents double-booking, so a slot that's already taken (aircraft or instructor) isn't
offered. Rooms and other resources can be attached to a booking too.

## Make a booking

1. Click a free slot on the calendar.
2. Choose the **booking type** — the form adapts to it (lesson, rental, trial flight, ground
   school, sim, check flight, maintenance, event…).
3. Set the time, the resource (instructor/aircraft), and the student or pilot.
4. If they hold an **hour package**, a picker lets you draw it down.
5. Create it — it lands on the calendar and dashboards immediately.

## Student self-booking

Students (and qualified pilots) can request their own slots from **My Calendar**. A request
arrives as **pending** for the office to **confirm** or decline; the student is notified either
way. See the [student manual](../roles/student.md#request-a-lesson-or-booking).

## Flight close-out

When an aircraft flight is done, **close it out** to capture the actuals:

- A live **Start / End timer** records actual start/end and landings while the flight is in
  progress.
- The front desk can close out any airframe flight for hours.
- **Multi-leg flights** (a cross-country that lands away and returns) are logged as one hire
  made of several **sectors**, each with its own route, times and landings, that add up to the
  total.

## Cancelling

Open the booking and cancel it, subject to your school's rules. Group bookings (ground school /
sim with several students, or multi-person events) cancel/edit together.
