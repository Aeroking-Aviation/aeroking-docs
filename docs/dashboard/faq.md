# Dashboard FAQ

Common questions about the AeroKing Dashboard. Use the **search box** at the top of the
page to jump to a topic, or browse the sections below.

## Signing in & accounts

??? question "I didn't get my invitation / password email"
    Check your spam folder first. If it's still missing, the email on file may be wrong —
    ask your school's administrator to confirm your address and resend the invite. See
    [Getting started](../getting-started.md#first-time-sign-in).

??? question "I've forgotten my password"
    On the login screen select **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and follow the reset
    link. See [Getting started](../getting-started.md#forgotten-password).

??? question "What's my school's web address?"
    Each school has its own portal, like `https://<your-school>.aerokingaviation.com`. Your
    school sends you the exact link — bookmark it.

??? question "How do I change my phone number / address / emergency contact?"
    Select **your name** (top right) → **My Settings**. You can edit your own profile there
    at any time.

## What I can see and do

??? question "Why is my menu different from a colleague's with the same role?"
    AeroKing shows only the areas you have permission ([capability](reference/capabilities.md))
    to use. An owner may have granted your colleague an extra capability — so their menu has
    an item yours doesn't. This is expected.

??? question "A menu I used to see has disappeared"
    Usually one of two things: an administrator changed your permissions, or an **add-on**
    (like Handling) was switched off for the school. Check with your administrator.

??? question "What's the difference between a role and a capability?"
    Your **role** is who you are (student, instructor, staff…); **capabilities** are the
    individual things you're allowed to do. Your role sets sensible defaults, and
    capabilities can be fine-tuned per person. See the
    [overview](overview.md#core-concepts).

## Bookings

??? question "I requested a booking — why isn't it confirmed?"
    A request starts as **pending** until the school approves it. You'll get a notification
    when it's confirmed or declined. Confirmed bookings appear on your calendar.

??? question "Why can't I book a particular slot?"
    AeroKing prevents double-booking: if the aircraft or instructor is already booked for
    that time, the slot isn't available. Try another time, aircraft or instructor.

??? question "How do I cancel a booking?"
    Open the booking from your calendar and cancel it, if your school's cancellation rules
    allow. If the option isn't there, contact the front desk.

## Training & logbook

??? question "My progress didn't update after a lesson"
    Progress updates when your instructor **submits the assessment** for that lesson. If
    it's been a while, give your instructor a nudge — grading is their step.

??? question "Can I print my logbook?"
    Yes — open **Pilot's Logbook** and choose **print** for a clean, printable copy suitable
    for your paper logbook or a licence application.

## Money

??? question "Where do I see what I owe?"
    Open **My Account** for your balance and transaction history. For a formal breakdown,
    your school can produce your **statement**, which is the definitive record of charges,
    payments and credit.

??? question "How do gift vouchers work?"
    A voucher is sold against a product (e.g. a trial flight) and redeemed against a booking;
    its value is drawn down automatically. Staff and administrators manage vouchers under
    **Finance → Vouchers & Packages**.

??? question "Can you invoice a walk-in who isn't a member?"
    Yes — administrators and staff can raise an **ad-hoc invoice** for non-members from
    **Finance → Billing & Accounts**.

## Handling / visiting aircraft

??? question "I don't see the Handling menu"
    Handling / PPR is an **optional add-on**. If your school hasn't enabled it, the menu
    won't appear. An administrator can turn it on in School Settings.

??? question "How does a visiting pilot request to land?"
    They submit a request through the school's public **parking-request** link; it arrives
    in the Handling queue for staff to approve. See the [Staff manual](roles/staff.md#check-a-visiting-aircraft-in-and-out-handling).

## Technical

??? question "Does AeroKing work on a phone or tablet?"
    Yes. The menu collapses into a **☰** button; everything else adapts to the smaller
    screen.

??? question "I think something's broken / I have a suggestion"
    Use the **Feedback** button (bottom corner of most pages) to report it to the AeroKing
    team, or the **Assistant** for a quick how-to. You can also use the **Support** link in
    your portal footer.
